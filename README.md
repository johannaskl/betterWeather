# ☀️ Bättre Väder

**An app for people who love sunshine and want to choose when it rains.**

Bättre Väder is a small and playful React application where you get to control the weather yourself.

Not happy with the weather? Make it better.
Too hot? Make it worse.
Want sunshine? You've got it. ☀️

Because sometimes you just want to be in charge of the weather.

## 🌤️ Features

- Random starting temperature between 17°C and 28°C
- Sunny weather when the temperature is between 17°C and 30°C
- Maximum 10% clouds during the starting weather
- Increase the temperature by 1°C with the "Better weather" button
- Decrease the temperature by 1°C with the "Worse weather" button
- Temperature ranges from -15°C to 35°C
- Random wind speed
- Random cloud coverage as the weather gets worse
- Weather icons that change depending on the temperature and cloud coverage

## 🎮 How it works

When the app starts, you get a random sunny temperature between 17°C and 28°C.

## ☀️ Better weather

Click "Bättre väder" to increase the temperature by one degree.
The temperature can go up to 35°C. 🥵
The wind and cloud coverage are also randomized to make every click slightly different.

## ☔ Worse weather

Click "Sämre väder" to decrease the temperature by one degree.
The temperature can go down to -15°C.🥶
As the temperature drops, the weather can become more cloudy and windy.

## 💡 The idea

The idea for this app came from a colleague who is never quite satisfied with the weather.
She wants sunshine, unless she's sick. Then rain is suddenly okay.

Real weather is something we cannot control.
This app is the opposite.
Here, you decide when the sun shines and when the rain comes. ☀️🌧️
